Snapbook

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Snapbook is a flag football playbook editor that runs entirely in your browser. There is no Snapbook account and no Snapbook server that receives your data. Questions or requests can be raised on the GitHub issue tracker.

Data stored on your device

Your playbook, roster, route library, wrist-coach settings and route-tree options are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on that device until you clear site data, use the app's reset options, or replace them by importing a playbook.

Dropbox, only if you connect it

If you choose to connect Dropbox, Snapbook asks Dropbox for four permissions:

  • account_info.read — your account's basic information (name and email), used only to label the connection in the app.
  • files.content.read and files.content.write — read and write files inside its own app folder.
  • files.metadata.read — read metadata about that folder.

It writes a single file, snapbook.json, at Apps/Snapbook/snapbook.json and reads it back. Snapbook cannot see or change anything else in your Dropbox. The file is transferred directly between your browser and Dropbox; no server operated by Snapbook is involved.

When you disconnect, Snapbook forgets the Dropbox access tokens stored in your browser. The snapbook.json file remains in your Dropbox until you delete it.

Share links

A share link carries the playbook in the URL fragment, which the recipient's browser decodes. Nothing is uploaded to create a link. Anyone you send the link to can read that playbook, so treat the link itself as the share.

No tracking

Snapbook has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images.

Deleting your data

Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored locally. To remove the Dropbox copy, delete snapbook.json from the app folder, or revoke Snapbook in your Dropbox account's connected-apps settings.
